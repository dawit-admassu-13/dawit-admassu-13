# Hi, I'm Dawit Zeleke 👋

**PhD Researcher (IT) · AI, RAG & Distributed Systems · AI-Cloud Architecture · Software Engineer, 10+ years**

I'm a software engineer with 10+ years of industry experience and a PhD researcher in Information Technology at the University of the Cumberlands. My work connects hands-on engineering of distributed, cloud-based enterprise systems with research on AI-cloud architecture, retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) reliability, and the security of distributed and multi-cloud systems.

---

## 🔬 Research Focus

- **AI systems reliability** — failure modes in retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) pipelines
- **AI-cloud architecture** — cloud solutions for AI workloads such as supply-chain demand forecasting
- **Distributed and multi-cloud security** — Zero Trust, anomaly-based detection, federated learning, and risks in AI-agent-supported systems
- **Cloud infrastructure performance** — virtualization and IaaS optimization
